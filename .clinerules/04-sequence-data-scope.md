# Sequence Data Scope

The application works primarily with nucleotide sequences.

Expected capabilities include:
- FASTA parsing and export.
- GenBank parsing and export.
- Sequence metadata.
- Sequence length.
- GC content.
- Nucleotide composition.
- Ambiguous nucleotide detection.
- Reverse complement.
- Pairwise sequence comparison.
- Sequence identity.
- Distance calculations.
- Sequence alignment.
- Quality-control metrics.
- Visualization.
- Export of analysis results.
